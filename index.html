2026 — now

Building Didao

Real conversations don’t wait until you’re fluent. Didao lets you use the Chinese, or the English, you already know: its helper DaoDao finds natural wording while you’re texting or talking, explains tone and usage, and turns the expressions you actually needed into practice. It’s built around learning from what you’re trying to say. Waitlist open; iOS and Android coming soon.
